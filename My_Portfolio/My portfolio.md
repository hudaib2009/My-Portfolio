## Ongoing Research.

Ongoing research · Biomedical Engineering / AI

### AI-Guided Nanomaterial-Based Electronic Nose for Early Lung Cancer Detection Using Multivariate Breath VOC Signatures

#### Overview

A research direction exploring how nanomaterial-based sensor arrays, breath volatile organic compound (VOC) signatures, signal processing and machine learning could be combined for early lung cancer detection.

#### Motivation

Early detection of lung cancer matters for outcomes, and breath analysis is a non-invasive direction worth investigating.

#### Objectives

- Study breath VOC signatures as multivariate data
- Explore sensor-array response patterns
- Evaluate ML approaches for pattern recognition

#### Proposed approach

- Nanomaterial sensor arrays
- VOC analysis
- Signal processing and feature extraction
- Machine-learning classification

#### Status

Ongoing and exploratory. No working sensor, validation results or clinical claims exist at this stage.
